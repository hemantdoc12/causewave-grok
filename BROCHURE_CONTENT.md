- **Logo:** Use `logo_2.png` (navbar version) for header, `logo_1.png` (hero version) for cover

## DESIGN TIPS FOR CANVA

1. **Start with a template:** Search "A4 brochure" or "NGO brochure" in Canva
2. **Replace colors:** Use the sky/teal/emerald palette above
3. **Upload logos:** Download `logo_1.webp` and `logo_2.webp` from the website
4. **Use icons:** Canva has built-in icons — search for "check", "handshake", "chart", etc.
5. **QR code:** Generate at qr-code-monkey.com, download PNG, add to last page
6. **Export:**
   - Print: Download as PDF (Print quality)
   - Digital: Download as PDF (Standard quality) or PNG
